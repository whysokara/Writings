I once read about a horse named Zippy Chippy.
He wasn’t like the other racehorses. While most were remembered for their wins, Zippy Chippy became famous for something completely different.. he just kept losing.

Race after race, over a hundred times, he showed up... and he lost.
But the crazy part? People loved him for it.

They didn’t come to see him win. They came to see him try, to laugh a little, to cheer for someone who had no real shot but still ran anyway.

Over time, Zippy Chippy wasn't just a racehorse anymore. He was a reminder that sometimes, the fight matters more than the finish line.
And somehow, I can’t help but think of RCB.

Every IPL season, there’s this familiar feeling.
The auctions, the hype videos, the star-studded lineups.
Kohli looking more determined than ever, the fans screaming, "Ee Sala Cup Namde!"

And then... well, you know how it goes.

A couple of heartbreaks later, it’s memes, tears, and that old friend called hope.
The funny thing, just like Zippy Chippy, the losses almost make RCB even more lovable.

The fans don’t leave. 
If anything, they come back louder, crazier, and a little more delusional every year.

Maybe one day, RCB will finally lift the trophy.
Or maybe, lifting it would break the magic.

Because let’s be honest .. if RCB ever actually wins, half their fanbase won’t know whether to celebrate or file for emotional unemployment.
