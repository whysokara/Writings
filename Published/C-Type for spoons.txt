Don’t you all think we need a standard spoon by now? Like genuinely, what is this “ye plastic spoon se kha lo”? Are kyu khalo? We should have a proper body that handles this, just the way the world agreed on C-type for almost every device. We need a mutually accepted global spoon. And it should be steel.

We go out to eat and they hand you a plastic spoon that bends even before it touches your food item. Wooden spoon bhi nahi chahiye. I will save the environment somewhere else. First give me a proper steel spoon.

You may argue that small restaurant owners prefer plastic spoons because washing steel spoons incurs labour charges. But come on, you already pay GST, SGST, convenience fee, service charge, pata nahi kya kya. Add a tiny steel spoon tax. Add a rupee and move on.

You may also argue that there will be hygiene issues, ki spoon thik se dhule hai ya nahi. If that is the concern, then carry your own suit when you go for paintball or roam around with your helmet all day because you never know when you will need to take a Rapido.

So let’s just decide how our ideal steel spoon should look. Not too small, not too big. Not paper-light either, this matters. A little weight in cutlery makes it feel real.

Nothing fancy. Just a simple, reliable, standardized steel spoon that doesn’t behave like a disposable toy.
