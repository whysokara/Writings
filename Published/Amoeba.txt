Life is a circle?

No dude.
Life is anything but a circle.

If it were, I would know what is coming. I would sense the turn before it arrives. Same radius. Same rhythm. Same comfort of repetition. Circles are polite. They do not surprise you.

Life does.

no fixed angle. no smooth curves promised. One day you move forward, the next you slide sideways, sometimes nowhere at all. Not because you miscalculated. Because there was nothing to calculate.

Life is an amoeba.

no permanent shape. It stretches toward what feels right. Pulls back when it must survive. does not follow geometry. It listens. Food here. Danger there. It reshapes itself in real time.

So do we.

Yes, life can loop back. You meet old places, old people, old versions of yourself. But the curve is never the same.

A circle is predictable.
An amoeba is alive.

And maybe that is the point.

Life does not move to complete a shape.
It moves to stay alive.

Tomorrow, it may look nothing like today.
