Indian women’s team won the World Cup, and now it is everywhere.
Reels, stories, headlines.

Funny thing? A week ago, half of us didn’t even know the tournament schedule.

But that is how the world claps: 
only when the curtain drops and the trophy shines.

No one is obligated to care about your journey while you are still building.
There are 8 billion lives in motion, everyone is busy surviving their own story.
You can’t expect the world to pause for your group stage.

People show up when you win, not when you are trying. 
And honestly, thik bhi hai. koi kyu krega?

So don’t crave applause during the climb. Earn it at the top.
And when cheers finally come, don’t mistake celebration for loyalty.
Most are here for the victory, not the journey.

But still… what a win. 💙
Go Win People.
