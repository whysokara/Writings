apple wants me to update my device..this time, for something called Apple Intelligence.
Honestly, I’d be more tempted if it came with a bit of artificial dumbness.

Somewhere along the way, our devices got too clever for their own good. Everything’s optimised, suggested, auto-filled, nudged. It’s exhausting. We don't need our tech to be dumb-dumb, just a little less needy.

Artificial dumbness isn't about going backward. It’s about giving our heads a little space. About designing for pause. For days when nothing is tracked and somehow everything still feels right. Not everything needs to be optimised. Not every moment needs a prompt, a ping, or a predictive text box. 

But yes! Google Maps can stay.
