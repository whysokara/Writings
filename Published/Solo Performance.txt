At every sangeet, there’s always that one guys’ dance. Zero prep, no matching outfits, steps all over the place, and a song chosen in pure panic. It’s messy in the most honest way.

But the cheer they get is always the loudest. 

Maybe that’s the point. Life doesn’t reward perfection as much as it rewards people who show up, have fun, and don’t overthink every beat. The ones who let go a little end up stealing the moment without trying.

We’re all chasing perfect choreography. Most days, it’s enough to just get on the stage and dance. And if all else fails, there’s always that one signature move every guy relies on anyway.
