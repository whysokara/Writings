When you are about to cry and your eyes fill with those first tiny tears, there is a moment when you suddenly start seeing everything in 4K.

It’s as if the universe pauses and hands you one last clear frame before the blur arrives. For a second, light looks softer, details feel sharper, and the truth you have been avoiding stands quietly in front of you.

That brief clarity is the universe’s way of saying, let it sink in, because the next moment will wash it all into softness.
