When I see Diwali, I see everything I want.
I see people coming home. Not because they have to,
But because something pulls them back.
The ones working in different cities, 
Living in rented apartments, eating alone every day
They all find their way back.
No excuses. No maybes.
They just come home.

I see real happiness.. the kind you can't fake.
Not the kind we show on social media,
But the happiness that comes
When someone's mother feeds them after months,
When old friends sit in a circle and forget time,
When the house fills with noise and chaos,
I see homes coming alive. I see houses turning into homes.
Every door is open, every home welcomes guests.
Food gets shared, stories get told,
Nobody wants to leave early, Nobody feels alone.
Mothers smiling at a house that finally feels full. 
For one evening, everything is as it should be.

I want this power that Diwali has
To make busy people pause,
To make tired people smile,
To make lonely people feel found.
To make everyone remember
That they belong somewhere,
That they matter to someone.

I want what Diwali has.
Not the lights, sweets or new clothes,
But this power to make everyone feel at home.
Because Diwali shows us what we're missing:
People who love us,
A place where we belong,
A reason to pause,
A reason to return,
A reason to stay.
