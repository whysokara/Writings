• The neighbour you only meet when the garbage truck arrives and both of you step out half asleep

These things don’t need effort. They grow on repetition and quiet recognition. On days when things feel heavy, even that simple nod from a “Kachra gadi friend” can steady you for a moment. These little bonds don’t care about age, gender, or social and economic status.

It’s just a small reminder that some parts of your day are familiar and easy, without you trying.
